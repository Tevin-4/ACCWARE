Focus on Your Projects. Accware Will Manage Your ERP.

ASSep 2, 20266 min read

Construction companies operate in demanding environments where every project must be carefully managed against budgets, timelines, materials, labour, and contractual obligations. Yet many construction firms do not have a full-time project administrator that helps a business keep everything in one place.

Site engineers and project managers naturally focus on execution: mobilising teams, supervising work, resolving site challenges, and delivering projects.

Consequently, important administrative tasks such as monitoring project costs, maintaining accurate data, reviewing commitments, and tracking overall project health may receive less attention.

This is where Accware Solutions comes in.

We provide ERP managed services that help construction companies keep their systems, project information, financial records, and operational processes accurate and reliable.

Your team remains focused on building and delivering projects while we manage the supporting ERP environment.

How Accware Solutions Helps Your Construction Business

Accware Solutions acts as an extension of your team. We handle the day-to-day administration, monitoring, support, and improvement of your ERP system.

Project Setup and Cost Management

We help establish projects correctly in the ERP system, including project structures, budgets, cost codes, tasks, and reporting requirements. Proper setup gives management a reliable foundation for monitoring costs, commitments, progress, and profitability.

Purchasing and Inventory Oversight

We support the management of purchase orders, material requests, inventory transactions, and supplier commitments. This improves visibility over what has been ordered, received, issued to projects, and charged to each job.

Day-to-Day ERP Administration

Our team handles routine ERP administration, system configuration, user support, and process guidance. We help users complete their work correctly and resolve operational issues before they disrupt the business.

Financial Period-End Support

We assist finance teams with period-end reviews, system checks, reconciliations, and reporting. This helps management receive timely and dependable financial information for decision-making.

Data Quality Checks and System Audits

Poor-quality data leads to unreliable reports. We conduct regular checks to identify missing, duplicated, inconsistent, or incorrectly classified information and recommend corrective action.

Reports, Dashboards, and Business Intelligence

Accware Solutions develops customised reports, management dashboards, and business intelligence tools. These provide clearer visibility into project costs, budget performance, purchasing, inventory, cash flow, and overall business performance.

User Training and Knowledge Transfer

We train employees to use the system confidently and correctly. We also provide practical guidance and knowledge transfer so that your team understands both the system and the business process it supports.

Monitoring and Proactive Support

We monitor the system to identify performance issues, errors, and potential risks early. This enables us to address problems proactively instead of waiting for them to interrupt operations.

Maintenance, Upgrades, and Security

Our services can include patches, upgrades, bug fixes, access-control management, vulnerability management, and compliance support. We help keep your system secure, stable, and up to date.

Backup and Business Continuity Support

We help protect critical business information through backup and disaster-recovery support. This reduces the risk of data loss and improves your organisation's ability to continue operating after an unexpected disruption.

System Integration Support

We support integrations between your ERP and other business applications. This helps information move efficiently between departments and reduces unnecessary manual data entry.

Process Improvement

Technology delivers the most value when it supports efficient processes. We review how your organisation uses its ERP system and recommend improvements that increase control, visibility, productivity, and return on investment.

Benefits of ERP Managed Services

Working with Accware Solutions can help your construction company:

  • Reduce the administrative burden on project, finance, and IT teams.
  • Access specialised ERP expertise without employing a large internal support team.
  • Improve project-cost tracking and visibility.
  • Increase system uptime, reliability, and performance.
  • Strengthen data security and access controls.
  • Produce more accurate and timely management reports.
  • Resolve user and system issues more quickly.
  • Plan operating costs through an agreed service arrangement.
  • Focus internal resources on construction and other core business activities.
  • Maximise the long-term value of its ERP investment.
